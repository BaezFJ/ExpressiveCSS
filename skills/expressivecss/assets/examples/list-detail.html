Saving seeds for spring

Leave a few of your healthiest bean pods on the plant until they turn brown and dry. Open them over a tray and spread the seeds in one layer.

After a week in a dry, airy spot, put them in a paper envelope. Write the variety, the harvest date and the plot number on the front. Next spring, another grower can pick up where you left off.

Push a finger into the soil before reaching for the hose. If it feels cool and damp below the surface, the bed can wait.

Water near the roots early in the morning. A layer of leaf mulch helps the soil hold moisture, giving the next volunteer less to carry on a hot afternoon.

A first visit is easier when someone knows your name. Meet new volunteers at the gate and show them where the tools and drinking water are.

Offer a choice of jobs, including one that can be done sitting down. Ask what they would enjoy learning, then work alongside them for the first few minutes.

Why these treatments?

Restrained suits scanning a reference collection. Expressive suits a short community reading session where one selected story deserves attention. Item order, content and read state remain identical.

  • Action size: medium Mark as read becomes large. List rows and Back remain at least 48px high without competing with that action.
  • Containment: canonical list-detail panes show one pane below 840px. Expressive contains only the selected story's introduction; body copy stays in an open reading column.
  • Typography: the selected story headline becomes headline-large at weight 500. List labels and body copy keep their scale and useful line lengths.
  • Color: primary-container/on-primary-container distinguishes the expressive story introduction. Selected-row color is accompanied by aria-current; read state is explicit text.
  • Shape: the story introduction changes from 16px to 32px corners. Its action receives the scoped 16px corner choice. Reading-list rows keep a consistent shape.

No framework JavaScript is needed for panes or lists. The application moves the active pane and keyboard focus, retains selection across resizing, and keeps reading status in memory.
