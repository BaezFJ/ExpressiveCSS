Why these treatments?

Restrained fits a workspace people revisit to make small changes. Expressive fits a first visit where choosing a manageable routine is the main task. Neither mode changes the options or their saved meaning.

  • Action size: medium Save in restrained; large Save in expressive. Every interactive target remains at least 48px high.
  • Containment: a quiet surface groups the introduction; expressive gives that same region a stronger boundary. Fields remain in native fieldsets.
  • Typography: headline-medium becomes headline-large, with a scoped weight of 500. Labels keep the same readable scale and bundled Roboto.
  • Color: surface-container-low/on-surface becomes primary-container/on-primary-container only in the introduction. Save keeps the primary/on-primary pair. Error roles are never decoration.
  • Shape: the focal region changes from 16px to 32px corners, with Save at 16px through its scoped container-shape token. These are application choices, not a common-button morph API.

No framework JavaScript is needed here. The sample handles native form submission in memory; it sends no email and writes no account settings.
