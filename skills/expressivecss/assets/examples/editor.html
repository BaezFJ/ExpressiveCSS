Why these treatments?

Restrained suits a coordinator drafting updates every week. Expressive suits a community workshop where the preview step helps first-time writers check their work. The same subject, message and formatting survive switching treatments.

  • Action size: medium Preview becomes large; the secondary Save draft stays medium. Preview is the focal action because this sample cannot publish.
  • Containment: the introduction is one contained region. The writing area stays open and outlined; the preview has a separate reading boundary.
  • Typography: expressive enlarges only the page headline and sets its weight to 500. Editor labels and newsletter body remain stable.
  • Color: primary-container/on-primary-container marks the expressive introduction. Tonal formatting buttons remain secondary, and their pressed state also changes shape.
  • Shape: expressive increases the introductory corners and scopes Preview's primary-action corner token. Connected toggles use their documented runtime shapes in both modes.

Only the formatting group needs framework initialization. The sample uses a textarea and textContent, not HTML editing. Preview formatting applies to the whole message, and Save draft stores it in memory.
