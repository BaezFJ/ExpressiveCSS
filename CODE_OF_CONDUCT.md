# Code of conduct

We welcome participants of every background and experience level. Treat people
with respect, make room for newcomers, and give feedback about the work rather
than the person. Respect people's names, pronouns, privacy, and time.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention, personal attacks,
and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. These
rules apply in project spaces and when representing ExpressiveCSS elsewhere.

Report incidents privately to baezdevs@gmail.com. Include links or other evidence
when available. BaezFJ handles reports confidentially, shares information only as
needed to investigate, and explains the outcome to affected participants when possible.

Depending on severity and repetition, moderation may involve a request to stop,
removing content, a warning, temporary restrictions, or removal from the community.
Immediate restrictions may be necessary to protect participants. Appeals can be
sent to the same address with new evidence. During the single-maintainer stage,
there is no independent appeal panel; reports involving the maintainer can also
be escalated through GitHub's platform abuse reporting process.
