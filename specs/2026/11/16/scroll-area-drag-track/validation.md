The hit test read an element box that jsdom doesn't have and left the 2px beside the thumb undefined (R4, R10, V2, V3, V8). A zero travel divided by zero (R4, V2). An unregistered custom property doesn't compute to `18px`, and a unitless offset broke `max()` on pages without the bar (R13, V9). V9's unscrollable arm and its click on the bar depended on unstated state. Unprefixed `user-select` was dropped in favor of the behavior check (R1, V6). Clauses without a check gained one: the context menu, Ctrl with the primary button, pen and touch, the state before `init`, coarse pointer and print, a smooth track step, the start clamp, the third cause in R9, and a wheel scroll during a drag. Corrected claims: `backToTop.ts`, the scrollspy selector, the specificity tie, the test titles R17 changes, the other edge content in Risks, and the tests V13 names.
- Rejected, 3 suggestions. V1's `tabindex` read and V6's wheel read pass today; they stay because they guard regressions this phase could cause. Setting `scrollY` to 900 in V8 after the hold is redundant and harmless, and it keeps the row readable on its own. V14 stays not required for merge, because the project requires no human check for merge.
