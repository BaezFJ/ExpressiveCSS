# Scroll area horizontal bar: requirements

- Feature: `ScrollArea` draws a second bar along the block-end edge for content that overflows sideways, on the page and on containers
- Date: 2026-11-16
- Roadmap phase: [Phase 102: add the horizontal bar](../../../../roadmap.md#phase-102-add-the-horizontal-bar), the fourth phase of the milestone "custom scrollbars". It depends on phase 101. The [mission](../../../../mission.md#product-rules) keeps markup compatibility and accessibility contracts stable, makes behavior changes opt-in, and lets WCAG win over other guidance. The [tech stack](../../../../tech-stack.md#framework-stack) asks for native platform features before script, with a fallback where an engine lacks one.
- Branch: `feature/2026-11-16-milestone-custom-scrollbars`. The milestone ships as one pull request, which the milestone run owns, so this phase adds no branch.
- Base: `d039e1e96cf36401419117df8ce53d3b27f5d0d0`. Phases 99, 100 and 101 are built, reviewed and pushed at this commit and merge with the rest of the milestone. Their specs, [page bar](../scroll-area-page-bar/requirements.md), [drag and track](../scroll-area-drag-track/requirements.md) and [container bar](../scroll-area-container-bar/requirements.md), are the accepted baseline here. The roadmap keeps their unfinished labels until the merge.

## Current behavior and need

`ScrollArea` holds one bar element, `_bar` (`src/ts/components/scrollArea.ts:7`). Its metrics read `clientHeight`, `scrollHeight` and `scrollTop` (`:233`), its hit test reads `clientY` (`:160`), and its drag and track press set only `top` (`:162`, `:217`). The component reads the inline axis in two places: `_saveState` keeps `scrollLeft` across the wrap (`:105`), and the container's wheel listener forwards `deltaX` (`:79`) after testing the inline range for travel (`:82` to `:89`).

The native bars hide on both axes at once, because `scrollbar-width: none` has no per-axis form. That leaves two gaps:

- On the page, `html.scroll-area.custom:has(> .scroll-area-bar)` hides the native bars whenever the bar element exists (`src/sass/utilities/_scroll-area.scss:65`). A page that overflows sideways therefore has no bar for that axis.
- On a container, the native bars hide while the vertical bar is displayed (`_scroll-area.scss:78`). A container that overflows both ways has no bar for the inline axis, and a container that overflows only sideways keeps its native bar. Phase 101 recorded this as item 11 of its R22 and deferred it to this phase.

Wheel, keys and touch still scroll sideways in both cases, but a pointer user has nothing to drag and nothing shows the position.

## Scope

- A second bar in `ScrollArea` for the inline axis, on the page path and the container path: the element, the metrics, the thumb's tracking, the drag, the track press and the wheel listener on a container's bar.
- The corner: both bars end before the square where they would meet.
- Styles for the horizontal bars and the corner in `src/sass/utilities/_scroll-area.scss`, and a wider selector for hiding a container's native bars.
- One changed declaration in each of `_back-to-top.scss` and `_fab-menu.scss`, so both controls keep clear of the page's horizontal bar.
- The docs page, `llm.md`, the hand-written skill reference, the `semantics.json` note, the CHANGELOG entry and gzip budget lines.
- jsdom and browser tests in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.

## Exclusions

- Hover and pressed appearance (phase 103), idle hiding (104), marks (105) and the drag label (106).
- Repeating a held track press, and a press that jumps the thumb to the pointer. Phase 100 excluded both.
- A scroll timeline for a container's thumbs, which phase 101 excluded.
- Vertical writing modes. The horizontal bar is on the physical bottom edge and its thumb moves on the x axis, as phase 101 fixed the vertical bar to the y axis.
- A page whose `dir` is set only on `<body>`. The page bars read `<html>`, as phases 99 and 100 documented.
- New padding or insets for block-end content other than `.back-to-top` and `.fab-menu`. See the decision below.
- Observation of a page's sideways overflow that resizes neither `<html>` nor `<body>`. `refresh()` covers it (R13).
- New options, events or public methods.
- Roadmap completion state, which the milestone records after merge.

## Technical constraints

- The horizontal bar reuses the bar element's listeners, the measurement queue, the observers and the drag record of phases 99 to 101. The phase adds no second `ResizeObserver`, `MutationObserver` or `scroll` listener.
- The page bar's rule `html.scroll-area.custom > .scroll-area-bar` keeps its declarations in one rule. `tests/scroll-area-bar-browser.test.js:78` finds it by `position: fixed` and reads `touch-action` from the same body. Horizontal rules are separate rules placed after it, and they don't repeat `position: fixed`.
- The rule `html.scroll-area.custom:has(> .scroll-area-bar)` keeps its selector and body. `tests/shadow-dom.test.js:85` pins both.
- The forced-colors, coarse-pointer and print rules keep their selectors. `tests/forced-colors.test.js:19` matches one of them by a regular expression.
- No horizontal rule declares `display`. Both bars take it from the existing rules, so every fallback that hides the vertical bar hides the horizontal one.
- Listeners take `this._events.signal`. The component reads the document and window through `this.el.ownerDocument`.
- Each new function stays below cyclomatic complexity 5 (the fallow pre-commit gate), and the phase exports nothing new.
- A custom property that only script sets needs a `var()` fallback, or `tests/tokens.test.js` fails.
- The bars hold no text or icon, so their sizes stay in pixels.
- jsdom 31 has no layout, and `getBoundingClientRect()` returns zeros. A jsdom case that needs a corner supplies the bar width through whatever the implementation reads, and without that the corner is 0, so the single-axis numbers of the existing jsdom cases don't change.
- Tests run against the built bundles and tear instances down in `finally` blocks.

## Requirements

"The drawn bar's media conditions" means `screen and (forced-colors: none) and (not (pointer: coarse))`. "Displayed" means the bar has a box: those conditions hold and the bar lacks `hidden`, and on the page no modal dialog is open.

Structure

- **R1** A page instance appends two bars to `<html>`, the vertical bar and then `<div class="scroll-area-bar horizontal" aria-hidden="true">` holding one `<div class="scroll-area-thumb">`, as its last two children. A container instance's wrapper holds the container, the vertical bar and then the horizontal bar. The horizontal bar and its thumb have no role, no `tabindex` and no ARIA attribute other than `aria-hidden`, and neither is focusable. The component writes nothing new on the scroller. An instance that adds nothing under phase 101's R2 adds neither bar. This replaces "one" bar in phase 99's R1 and phase 101's R1.
- **R2** `ScrollArea` gains no option, event or public method. `refresh()` measures both axes.

Geometry

- **R3** While it is displayed, the page's horizontal bar is `position: fixed`, lies against the window's block-end edge, is `var(--scroll-area-bar-size, 12px)` tall, and spans the window's inline size, less the corner of R6. It has the vertical page bar's `z-index`, `touch-action` and track color. Its thumb is 8px tall, centered in the bar, with fully rounded ends and the vertical thumb's color.
- **R4** While it is displayed, a container's horizontal bar is `position: absolute` and anchored to the container. Its box spans the inline range of the container's padding box, less the corner of R6, is `var(--scroll-area-bar-size, 12px)` tall, and lies against the padding box's block-end edge, inside the border. Phase 101's rules for the vertical bar hold for it:
  - It stays on the container's edge without script when layout moves or resizes the container, when an ancestor scrolls, and while the container is sticky (phase 101's R9).
  - It carries the container's computed `direction`, `color`, `color-scheme` and `z-index`, the three scroll area properties and the local thumb color token (phase 101's R6).
  - The scaffold's pane, a dialog's body, a pane's body and an author's positioned scroller clip it (phase 101's R24).
  - In the frame after the container loses its box, neither the bar nor its thumb has a painted area.
- **R5** On each axis the track's length is the scroller's client size, less the corner of R6, and never less than 0. The thumb's length is the track's length times client size over scroll size, never less than 48px and never more than the track. The thumb's offset from the track's start is the scroll progress times the track's length minus the thumb's length, with the progress clamped to 0 through 1. On the inline axis the progress is the absolute value of `scrollLeft` over `scrollWidth - clientWidth`. A 240px-wide container with 720px-wide content and no vertical overflow has an 80px horizontal thumb, a third of its track, and at the end of the content the thumb's end touches the track's end. With one bar displayed, the vertical numbers are those of phase 99's R5 and R6 and phase 101's R7. An instance that starts on a scroller already scrolled sideways shows the right offset before any scroll event.
- **R6** While both bars of an instance are displayed, the vertical bar ends one bar width before the block-end edge and the horizontal bar ends one bar width before the inline-end edge. The square between them, `var(--scroll-area-bar-size, 12px)` on each side, intersects neither bar's box and neither thumb's box at any scroll position, the four extremes included. A container 300px tall and 240px wide with content 900px by 720px then has a 288px vertical track with a 96px thumb and a 228px horizontal track with a 76px thumb. When only one bar is displayed, it spans its whole edge. The corner takes no pointer: a press there reaches the content under it and scrolls nothing. Each bar's length follows the other's display at the next measurement, and the thumb lengths and offsets follow in the same measurement. The track length the script uses equals the drawn bar's length at every measurement. That includes the first measurement after a bar that had no box, because of `hidden`, a modal dialog or the media conditions, is displayed again, and a bar width given in a unit other than pixels.
- **R7** The horizontal thumb starts at the inline-start end of its track. Under `dir="rtl"` on a container or an ancestor, and on `<html>` for the page, the thumb's right edge is at the track's right end while `scrollLeft` is 0, and it moves left as the content scrolls toward its end. The vertical bar is then on the left (phase 99's R4, phase 101's R6), and the corner is at the bottom left.
- **R8** The horizontal thumb follows the scroll position on the path its vertical thumb uses:
  - On the page, where `CSS.supports('animation-timeline: scroll()')` is true, a CSS animation on the root's inline scroll timeline moves the thumb and the component adds no `scroll` listener. Elsewhere the one passive window listener of phase 99's R7 writes both thumbs, at most once per animation frame.
  - On a container, the one passive `scroll` listener of phase 101's R8 writes both thumbs, in every engine.
  - Both paths give the same geometry within 1px. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the horizontal thumb still follows the scroll position.

Visibility and native bars

- **R9** The horizontal bar has the `hidden` attribute when the scroller can't scroll on the inline axis: `scrollWidth` is not greater than `clientWidth`, or its computed `overflow-x` is `hidden` or `clip` at a measurement. A container's horizontal bar is also hidden whenever phase 101's R11 hides the vertical bar for a reason other than the block axis: the container isn't visible, isn't rendered, or is no longer the wrapper's child. It loses the attribute at the next measurement that finds the axis scrollable. The vertical bar keeps its own conditions, and the two attributes are independent. While a modal dialog is open, neither page bar is displayed (phase 99's R10).
- **R10** Under the drawn bar's media conditions, a wrapped container that has the `custom` class has `scrollbar-width: none` and `display: none` on its `::-webkit-scrollbar` while at least one of its two bars lacks `hidden`. A container that overflows only sideways therefore shows the drawn horizontal bar and no native bar. When both bars have `hidden`, the container keeps the utility's native bars. This replaces the last sentence of phase 101's R12 and item 11 of its R22. The page rule of phase 99's R2 is unchanged: the native bars hide while the bar elements exist.
- **R11** A container's eligibility on both axes is measured while at least one of its drawn bars lacks `hidden`, so no native gutter exists under the drawn bar's media conditions while the script reads. When both bars have `hidden`, the script unhides one before it reads. One measurement reads the client sizes, scroll sizes and scroll offsets in that state, then commits both `hidden` attributes and writes both thumbs before paint, in that order. It writes a `hidden` attribute only when the value changes, so a container whose state is settled gets no `hidden` mutation from a measurement that a scroll queued, unless both bars are hidden. With classic native scrollbars:
  - Content that fits on both axes in the candidate state leaves both bars hidden and the native bars in place, and the result does not alternate between frames. That includes content that overflows one axis only because the other axis's native gutter takes space, and text whose reflow beside a native gutter creates overflow.
  - Content that overflows either axis in the candidate state shows the drawn bar for that axis only, with thumb, drag and track metrics from the client sizes read in that state.
  - While nothing changes, no measurement runs. This holds after a fit, after an overflow on one axis, after an overflow on both, and after a return to a fit.
- **R12** Without an instance, after `destroy()`, after `custom` is removed, under forced colors, with a coarse primary pointer, in print, and for a container in a window without anchor positioning, neither drawn bar is displayed and the scroller has the utility's native bars for both axes. Sideways scrolling by wheel, keys, touch and the native bar works in each case. Restoring `custom` or the media conditions shows the drawn bars again.
- **R13** The horizontal thumb's length and offset update without a scroll in the cases that update the vertical thumb: for the page, when `<html>` or `<body>` resizes, when the window resizes, and on `refresh()` (phase 99's R9), and for a container, in the cases of phase 101's R10. A change in one axis's overflow updates the other bar's length in the same measurement (R6). Page content that grows or shrinks sideways without resizing `<html>` or `<body>` is measured at the next of those events, and the docs name `refresh()` for it.

Pointer

- **R14** The horizontal bar takes the pointer as phase 100's R1, R2, R4 to R8 and R10 and phase 101's R14 specify, with the axes exchanged:
  - The hit test compares the pointer's `clientX`, measured from the bar's left edge, with the thumb's inline range, anywhere across the bar's height.
  - A press inside that range starts a drag. Each pointer move sets the scroller's inline scroll position to the position at the press plus the pointer's horizontal travel times the ratio recorded at the press, without animation, and leaves the block position alone. Moving the pointer right moves the thumb right in `ltr` and in `rtl`. Dragging through half of the thumb's travel scrolls half of the scrollable width. A drag past either end stops at that end.
  - A press outside that range scrolls by `clientWidth` once, toward the press, with the scroller's own `scroll-behavior`. Holding it doesn't repeat.
  - When the thumb has no travel, a press on it does nothing.
  - A primary press leaves focus and the selection alone, and the generated primary click is cancelled and stops bubbling. Other buttons, a press with Ctrl, and a second pointer do nothing.
  - A container's bar scrolls the container and never the page or an ancestor. The page's bar scrolls its window.

  The vertical bar's hit test, drag and track press give the same results as before, with the track length of R5.
- **R15** An instance has at most one drag across its two bars. A press on either bar while a drag is in progress does nothing. A drag ends on release, on `pointercancel`, on lost capture and when its own bar stops being displayed, by each cause in phase 100's R9 and phase 101's R16. After it ends, pointer moves scroll nothing until a new press. A drag on one bar continues when the other bar appears or hides, and it keeps the ratio recorded at the press. Phase 101's R16 limit holds for both bars as written there: a modal in a separate inaccessible closed shadow root that exposes no external focus transition needs owner coordination. Its owner destroys affected instances before `showModal()` and reinitializes them after closing, `refresh()` cannot detect or cancel that case, and watched containers opt out before destruction.
- **R16** With the pointer over a container's horizontal bar, a wheel scrolls the container as phase 101's R15 specifies, by the same rule for forwarding, cancelling, Ctrl and chaining. Neither page bar gets a `wheel` listener.

Clearance

- **R17** `.back-to-top` and `.fab-menu` read `--_page-bar-inset` for their block-end inset, with their offset property as the fallback, as phase 100's R13 defined for the inline-end inset. Where `--scroll-area-bar-clearance` is set, each control's block-end inset is the larger of its offset and the clearance: 18px by default, 19px beside a 14px bar and 30px beside a 30px bar, and an offset an author raised above the clearance is kept. Where the property is unset, the inset is the control's offset, a unitless `0` and a negative offset included. With both page bars displayed, the box of `.back-to-top` and of `.fab-menu` with its open menu each end at least 12px from each bar's center line, in `ltr` and `rtl`, and intersect neither bar nor the corner. A press anywhere in each control reaches it. The inset doesn't change when the horizontal bar gains or loses `hidden`. Controls inside a shadow root get the same insets. The clearance rule keeps its selector and body, and a container instance sets no clearance (phase 101's R13).

Lifecycle

- **R18** `destroy()` ends a drag on either bar and removes both bars with their listeners. For a container it restores the parent's nodes as phase 101's R17 specifies, with no wrapper or bar of that instance left in the document. `init` and `destroy()` keep the container's scroll offsets and focus as phase 101's R5 specifies, with its limit unchanged: focus returns to reachable targets, and a closed descendant root's owner restores its own focused leaf. Initializing twice, or `AutoInit()` after an explicit `init`, leaves one instance and one pair of bars. The page instance, several container instances, a container nested in another, an instance in an iframe and a container in a shadow root each drag only their own scroller on either axis (phase 101's R19). The registry and its selector don't change.

## Risks considered

- Two at once. A press on one bar during a drag on the other, and the other bar appearing mid-drag, are R15. Two `init` calls, AutoInit after `init`, nesting and iframes are R18. Both axes changing in one measurement is R6 and R11. `destroy()` during a drag is R18.
- Bad input. A scroller that can't scroll sideways, or has `overflow-x: hidden` or `clip`, is R9. A track shorter than 48px has a thumb as long as the track, so it has no travel and nothing to press, and wheel, keys and touch scroll it (R14). A container too small for both bars, narrower or shorter than the bar width plus 48px, gets the same treatment on that axis. `<body>`, a parentless element and a window without anchor positioning add nothing (R1).
- Refusal. The phase adds no permission or data boundary.
- Readers. The docs, `llm.md`, the skill reference, the semantics note, the budget lines and the pins are R19 and R20. Tests that locate the vertical bar by position or count are R21. `tests/shadow-dom.test.js:85` and `tests/forced-colors.test.js:19` pin rules the phase leaves alone (technical constraints). `_navbar.scss:505` reads the clearance for inline-end padding only and doesn't change. `BackToTop` and `ScrollSpy` watch the block axis through `IntersectionObserver`, which a sideways scroll doesn't trigger. The top app bar's scroll timeline reads the root's block axis.
- UI states. RTL is R7 and R17. Forced colors, coarse pointers, print, no runtime and no anchor positioning are R12, with native access on both axes. Reduced motion is R8. Without JavaScript there are no bars. Enlarged text widens content, which R13 measures. A modal dialog is R9 and R15.
- The native-gutter cycle. R11 keeps phase 101's fix and extends its candidate state to both bars. Plan group 3 watches the two-axis jsdom case fail and repeats the Chromium classic-scrollbar probe. Firefox and WebKit exposed no native gutter in phase 101's probe, so their results there don't establish classic-gutter behavior, and this phase claims none for them.
- Measurement cost. A measurement reads both axes in one layout pass, before it writes `hidden`, and R11 writes an attribute only on a change, so a settled container gets no attribute write per scroll frame. A container on which both bars are hidden still gets the unhide and the restore at each measurement, as a fitting container does in phase 101. On the listener paths a measurement runs at most once per frame, as before.
- Engine behavior without a check yet. The spec expects the root's inline timeline to run from the scroll start, at the right in RTL, to the scroll end, `scrollLeft` to be 0 at the start and negative toward the end in RTL, `anchor(self-start)` to follow the bar's own `dir`, and a computed `inline-size` to be in pixels for a bar without a box. Plan group 2 probes each one in every engine before the code that depends on it, and V2, V3 and V4 assert the results. If a probe disagrees, the requirement and its check change under "Decisions made while building" before the code is written.
- Content under the bar. The horizontal bar covers the last 12px of the scroller's block-end edge, as an overlay scrollbar does. On a system with classic scrollbars, content that fit above a native horizontal gutter now has its last 12px under the drawn bar. Item 7 of the container limits gives the padding (R19).
- Block-end page content. `.back-to-top` and `.fab-menu` are R17. The navigation bars, the docked and floating toolbars, the scaffold's trailing toolbar and the status bar are documented limits by the decision above, and phase 100's spacing rule is not met for them while the page overflows sideways (R19). An author's own block-end control uses the clearance property. A snackbar stacks above the bar: on a compact window it sits 8px from the edge and paints over the top 4px of the bar, and elsewhere it sits 24px up and clear. A non-modal bottom sheet stacks above the bar and covers it while it shows.
- Layout shift. `.back-to-top` and `.fab-menu` move 2px up once, when the page instance starts, together with the inline-end shift of phase 100. They don't move when sideways overflow starts or stops (R17).
- Stale sideways overflow on the page. Content wider than the body resizes neither observed box. R13 measures it at `init`, at a resize and on `refresh()`, and R19 documents the call. On the listener path the next scroll also measures.
- A 1px sideways overflow shows a horizontal bar with a thumb nearly as long as its track, as a native bar does.
- The corner. A press in the corner reaches the content under it (R6). With `--scroll-area-bar-size: 30px` the corner is 30px square, and the fixed controls clear it through the clearance (R17).
- Wheel over the horizontal bar. A vertical wheel there scrolls the container vertically, as it does over the content, because the listener forwards the event's own deltas (R16).
- Direction. The page bars read `dir` on `<html>`. A page that sets `dir` only on `<body>` isn't corrected, as phase 100 recorded. A container's bars read the container's computed direction at each measurement, and a stylesheet change to it shows at the next measurement or on `refresh()` (phase 101's R10).
- Stacking and clipping. The horizontal bar follows the vertical bar in tree order and takes the same `z-index`, so positive `z-index` content inside the container paints over both (item 5 of phase 101's R22). The framework's scrolling bodies clip both (R4).
- Rounded corners. A container's `border-radius` doesn't clip its sibling bars. The horizontal thumb at either end can cross a large radius by a few pixels, as phase 101 recorded for the vertical one.
- Observation cost. The phase adds no observer and no listener on the scroller (technical constraints).
- Pinch zoom. The page's horizontal bar stays with the layout viewport, as the vertical bar does.
- `specs/checklist.md`: the project has none.
